about unreader

unreader started as a way for me to read more books. it has now turned into this mission to help people learn better from the books they read.

at its core: the product lets you read and listen to the text you're reading. since this engages all your sense, you are more focused.

then it lets you ask questions. asking solves 2 things: you get to read more actively and you get to explore the angles that make you curious. it helps you go beyond the book or stay more grounded with it. you pick.

and finally, you interact with the book itself. you write your thoughts on a whiteboard. you write your takeaways to come back to. you write about what the book stirred in you.
